THE ALIEN

The most innocent girl I know.

Probably.

Because somehow that innocent girl has one of the darkest minds I've ever encountered.

She talks. A lot.

Sometimes about the most stupid things imaginable.

Sometimes in a way that somehow makes me feel like a kid.

She loves sarcasm.

She loves dark and creepy things.

And somehow she can make the word “sure” sound suspicious.

Definitely an alien.
