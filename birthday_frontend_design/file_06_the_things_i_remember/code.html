THE THINGS I REMEMBER

I look at you like a kid.

You're serious with everyone.

But with me...

You're a kid too.

And somehow, I'm even more of a kid.

You correct the way I talk.

The way I walk.

The way I sit.

The way I eat.

You tell me not to touch my own hair.

You tell me to make more friends.

You tell me:

“Stop doing that.”

“If you again do that I won't talk for a week.”

“Stop touching your ID card.”
